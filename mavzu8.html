22-§. Sutkalik va sutkalik-gorizontal parallaks. Quyosh sistemasi jismlarigacha masofani aniqlash

Sutkalik parallaks (p) — osmon yoritgichi markazidan Yer radiusining kuzatish nuqtasiga o‘tkazilgan kesmasi ko‘rinadigan burchak. Yoritgich ufqda (gorizontda, z = 90°) turganda Yerning ekvatorial radiusi (R⊕ = 6378.1 km) ko‘rinadigan burchak sutkalik-gorizontal parallaks (p₀) deyiladi (p = p₀ · sin z).

D = R⊕ / sin p₀ ≈ (206 265'' / p₀'') · R⊕  |  Radiolokatsiya usuli: D = (c · t) / 2
1-Masala: Marsning Buyuk ro‘para turishi paytida uning sutkalik-gorizontal parallaksi p₀ = 23.6'' ga teng bo‘ldi. Marsgacha bo‘lgan masofani (D) Yer radiuslari hisobida va kilometrlarda aniqlang (R⊕ = 6378 km).
1) Yer radiuslari hisobida: D = (206 265'' / 23.6'') · R⊕ ≈ 8740 R⊕.
2) Kilometrlarda: D = 8740 × 6378 km ≈ 5.574 × 10⁷ km (55.74 mln km).

23-§. Quyosh sistemasi jismlarining radiuslarini aniqlash

Osmon jismining ko‘rinma burchak radiusi ρ (burchak diametrining yarmi) va ungacha masofa D (yoki gorizontal parallaks p₀) ma’lum bo‘lsa, uning chiziqli radiusi R quyidagicha topiladi:

R = D · sin ρ ≈ D · (ρ'' / 206 265'')  |  Parallaks orqali: R = R⊕ · (ρ'' / p₀'')

2-Masala: Yupiterning ro‘para turishi paytida sutkalik-gorizontal parallaksi p₀ = 2.09'', ko‘rinma burchak diametri esa 46.8'' ekanligi o‘lchandi. Yupiterning ekvatorial radiusini (R_Yup) Yer radiuslari hisobida va kilometrlarda toping (R⊕ = 6378 km).
1) Burchak radiusi: ρ'' = 46.8'' / 2 = 23.4''.
2) Nisbiy radius: R_Yup = R⊕ · (23.4'' / 2.09'') ≈ 11.20 R⊕.
3) Kilometrlarda: R_Yup = 11.20 × 6378 km ≈ 71 434 km.
3-Masala: Veneraga yuborilgan radiosignal t = 280 s dan so‘ng Yerga qaytib keldi. Shu paytda Veneraning ko‘rinma burchak diametri 59.6'' bo‘lsa, Veneragacha masofani (D) va uning chiziqli radiusini (R_Ven) hisoblang (c = 3 × 10⁵ km/s).
1) Masofa: D = (3 × 10⁵ × 280) / 2 = 4.2 × 10⁷ km (42 mln km).
2) Burchak radiusi: ρ'' = 59.6'' / 2 = 29.8''.
3) Radius: R_Ven = 4.2 × 10⁷ × (29.8'' / 206 265'') ≈ 6068 km.
