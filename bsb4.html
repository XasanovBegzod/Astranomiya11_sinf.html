1-Yozma masala (67–70-§§ — Kosmik tezliklar va YSY orbitasi)

Yer radiusi R_⊕ = 6400 km. Sun’iy yo‘ldoshning perigey balandligi h_p = 400 km, apogey balandligi h_a = 1600 km. (a) Orbitaning katta yarim o‘qi (a) va ekssentrisitetini (e) hisoblang; (b) Agar perigey balandligi atmosfera qatlamiga kirib qolsa, apogey va perigey balandliklari vaqt o‘tishi bilan qanday o‘zgarishini tushuntiring.

(a) r_p = 6400 + 400 = 6800 km, r_a = 6400 + 1600 = 8000 km.
a = (6800 + 8000) / 2 = 7400 km; e = (8000 − 6800) / (8000 + 6800) = 1200 / 14800 ≈ 0.081.
(b) Perigeyda atmosfera qarshiligi tufayli tezlik kamayadi, natijada avval apogey balandligi (h_a) keskin pasayib, orbita aylanalashadi (e → 0), so‘ngra yo‘ldosh spiral bo‘ylab pastga tushadi.

2-Yozma masala (71–73-§§ — Orbital manyovrlar va Oyga uchish)

(a) Tezligi v = 7.6 km/s bo‘lgan sun’iy yo‘ldosh orbita tekisligini Δi = 10° burchakka burish uchun zarur tezlik impulsini (Δv_i) hisoblang (sin 5° ≈ 0.0872).
(b) Oyning Yer atrofida siderik davri T_Oy = 27.3 sutka. Agar kosmik apparat Oyga t = 3.5 sutka da yetib borsa, parvoz davomida Oy o‘z orbitasi bo‘ylab necha gradusga (Δφ) siljiydi?

(a) Δv_i = 2 · v · sin(Δi / 2) = 2 × 7.6 × sin 5° = 15.2 × 0.0872 ≈ 1.325 km/s = 1325 m/s.
(b) Δφ = 360° × (t / T_Oy) = 360° × (3.5 / 27.3) ≈ 46.2°.

3-Yozma masala (74–76-§§ — Gomon trayektoriyasi va Planeta tomon yaqinlashish)

Yer orbitasi radiusi r₁ = 1.0 a.b., Mars orbitasi radiusi r₂ = 1.52 a.b. (1.26^1.5 ≈ 1.414). (a) Gomon yarim ellipsi bo‘ylab Marsga uchish davomiyligini (t_parvoz) yil va sutkalarda hisoblang; (b) Mars ta’sir sferasiga v_∞ = 2.66 km/s nisbiy tezlik bilan kirgan apparat perisentrda (v₂,Mars = 5.0 km/s) qanday giperbolik tezlikka (v_p) ega bo‘ladi?

(a) a_o‘t = (1.0 + 1.52) / 2 = 1.26 a.b. ⇒ t_parvoz = 0.5 × 1.26^1.5 ≈ 0.5 × 1.414 = 0.707 yil ≈ 258 sutka.
(b) v_p = √(v₂,Mars² + v_∞²) = √(5.0² + 2.66²) = √(25 + 7.08) = √32.08 ≈ 5.66 km/s.
