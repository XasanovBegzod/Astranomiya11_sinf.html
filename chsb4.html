1-Yozma masala (33–34-mavzular — Kosmik tezlik va Ta’sir sferasi)

Mars sirtida birinchi kosmik tezlik v₁ = 3.55 km/s ga teng. (a) Mars sirtidagi ikkinchi kosmik tezlikni (v₂) hisoblang; (b) Agar sayyoraning Quyoshdan masofasi a_pl = 1.52 a.b. va (m_pl / M_☉)^0.4 ≈ 0.00253 bo‘lsa, ta’sir sferasi radiusi (R_ts) qanday fizik ma’noga ega?

(a) v₂ = √2 · v₁ ≈ 1.414 × 3.55 ≈ 5.02 km/s.
(b) Ta’sir sferasi (R_ts) ichida sayyoraning markaziy tortishish kuchi Quyoshning g‘alayonlantiruvchi ta’siridan ustun bo‘ladi.

2-Yozma masala (35-mavzu — Orbita elementlari va Atmosfera evolyutsiyasi)

Yer radiusi R_⊕ = 6400 km. Yo‘ldoshning perigey balandligi h_p = 300 km, apogey balandligi h_a = 1300 km. Orbitaning katta yarim o‘qini (a) va ekssentrisitetini (e) toping hamda atmosfera qarshiligi ta’sirida orbita shakli qanday o‘zgarishini tushuntiring.

r_p = 6700 km, r_a = 7700 km ⇒ a = (6700 + 7700)/2 = 7200 km; e = (7700 − 6700)/(7700 + 6700) ≈ 0.069.
Perigeyda tormozlanish tufayli apogey balandligi (h_a) tez kamayadi va orbita aylanalashadi (e → 0).

3-Yozma masala (36–37-mavzular — Tekislikni burish va Oyga parvoz)

(a) Aylana orbitada v = 7.5 km/s tezlik bilan harakatlanayotgan yo‘ldosh orbita tekisligini Δi = 6° burchakka burish uchun zarur impulsni (Δv_i) hisoblang (sin 3° ≈ 0.0523).
(b) Nima uchun kosmik apparat Oyga uchirilganda Oyning start paytidagi ko‘rinadigan o‘rniga emas, balki oldingi nuqtasiga yo‘naltiriladi?

(a) Δv_i = 2 · v · sin(Δi / 2) = 2 × 7.5 × 0.0523 ≈ 0.785 km/s = 785 m/s.
(b) Parvoz davomida (~3–4 sutka) Oy o‘z orbitasi bo‘ylab siljigani uchun uchrashuv vaqtida Oy yangi nuqtada bo‘ladi.

4-Yozma masala (38–40-mavzular — Gomon parvozi, Geliosentrik tezlik va Yaqinlashish)

Mars ta’sir sferasiga v_∞ = 3.0 km/s nisbiy tezlik bilan kirgan apparat perisentrda (v₂,Mars = 5.0 km/s) qanday giperbolik tezlikka (v_p) ega bo‘ladi? Agar perisentrda Δv_torm = 2.0 km/s tormozlanish impulsi berilsa, apparatning yangi tezligi nechaga teng bo‘ladi?

v_p = √(5.0² + 3.0²) = √34 ≈ 5.83 km/s.
Tormozlanishdan keyin: v_yangi = 5.83 − 2.0 = 3.83 km/s (parabolik tezlik 5.0 km/s dan kichik bo‘lgani uchun apparat Marsning yopiq orbitasiga o‘tadi).
